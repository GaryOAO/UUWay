# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want UUWay to be a welcoming, respectful project for everyone. Contributors
should be able to participate without harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks, or deliberately disruptive behaviour, regardless of background or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith while discussing technical decisions.
- Give feedback about code and ideas, not about a person's character.
- Explain the evidence behind a report, review, or proposal when possible.
- Accept that maintainers may make a different decision after considering the
  available evidence.
- Protect private information and remove credentials, account data, and
  unredacted logs from public issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualised language or imagery,
personal attacks, trolling, and deliberate disruption of project services or
discussion are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information
without permission is also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately by email to
`62834599+GaryOAO@users.noreply.github.com`. Please include a link or description
of the relevant material and any context needed to review it. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly as practical.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or
take other action appropriate to the situation. They will explain moderation
decisions when doing so does not expose private information or put someone at
risk.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and to conduct connected with
UUWay. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
